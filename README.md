## Power-BI Interactive Mobile Sales Dashboard | Data Science & Business Intelligence Project | Power BI

## Data Science

### 📌Project Overview

Developed an interactive **Mobile Sales Analytics Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI** to transform mobile retail sales data into meaningful business insights. The solution provides a consolidated view of revenue performance, sales volume, customer ratings, payment preferences, product demand, and geographic sales distribution.

The dashboard consists of three analytical pages: **Executive Sales Dashboard, Month-to-Date (MTD) Report, and Same Period Last Year Analysis**. Interactive filters, dynamic KPI cards, and time-intelligence calculations enable users to monitor business performance, identify sales trends, and support data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor key sales KPIs through a centralized reporting solution.
- Evaluate revenue contribution by brand, mobile model, and city.
- Analyze monthly sales trends, weekday performance, and product demand.
- Understand customer rating patterns and payment method preferences.
- Track cumulative Month-to-Date sales performance.
- Compare current-period sales against the corresponding prior-year period.
- Support business decisions related to inventory planning, marketing strategy, and sales performance optimization.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Identified major revenue-contributing brands, including Apple, Samsung, OnePlus, Vivo, and Xiaomi.
- Highlighted high-performing mobile models, including iPhone SE, OnePlus Nord, and Galaxy Note 20.
- Observed relatively stable monthly sales quantities, generally ranging from approximately 1.45K to 1.70K units.
- Identified July as the highest-performing month and February as the lowest-performing month by displayed sales quantity.
- Analyzed customer ratings, with feedback concentrated primarily in the Good and Average categories.
- Evaluated payment method distribution, which showed a relatively balanced transaction mix.
- Compared current-period sales with prior-year performance to assess changes in revenue and seasonality.

## ⚙️ Technical Implementation

**Data Preparation and Transformation**
- Imported and transformed mobile sales data using Power Query.
- Prepared data for consistent and reliable reporting.
- Validated data types and applied data-cleaning techniques.

**Data Modeling and DAX**
- Created measures for total sales, units sold, transactions, and average selling price.
- Implemented Month-to-Date calculations using DAX time-intelligence functions.
- Developed Same Period Last Year and year-over-year comparison measures.
- Applied filter context and date-based analysis to support interactive reporting.

**Dashboard Development**
- Designed a three-page analytical reporting experience.
- Built KPI cards, geographic maps, trend charts, and comparative visualizations.
- Implemented interactive slicers, cross-filtering, and page navigation.
- Enabled analysis by brand, mobile model, payment method, year, quarter, month, and day.
