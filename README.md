## Mobile Sales Performance & Revenue Analytics Dashboard

## Data Science

### **Microsoft Power BI | DAX | Power Query | Data Modeling | KPI Analysis | Sales Analytics**

## Project Overview

Developed an interactive **Mobile Sales Performance and Revenue Analytics Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI** to analyze retail sales transactions, revenue performance, customer preferences, product demand, and geographical sales distribution.

Designed a three-page analytical reporting solution that transforms sales data into interactive visualizations, business performance indicators, and actionable insights for retail decision-making.

## Business Problem

Mobile retailers need a centralized reporting system to monitor revenue, identify high-performing products, understand customer purchasing preferences, and evaluate historical sales performance.

This project addresses these analytical requirements by integrating sales performance monitoring, Month-to-Date (MTD) reporting, and Same Period Last Year comparisons into an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Project Objectives

- Monitor sales revenue, transaction volume, and product demand.
- Evaluate mobile brand and model performance.
- Analyze sales contributions across geographical markets.
- Examine customer ratings and payment preferences.
- Track Month-to-Date sales performance.
- Compare current sales against corresponding prior-year periods.
- Support data-driven inventory, marketing, and sales decisions.

## Project Highlights

1. **Data Cleaning & Transformation:** Imported and prepared mobile sales data using Power Query, validating data types and transforming records for analytical reporting.

2. **Data Modeling & DAX:** Developed measures for revenue, quantity, transactions, average selling price, and time-based sales comparisons.

3. **KPI Development:** Created interactive KPI cards to monitor essential retail sales performance indicators.

4. **Sales & Revenue Analysis:** Evaluated revenue distributions across brands, mobile models, cities, and reporting periods.

5. **Customer Behavior Analysis:** Examined customer rating distributions and payment method preferences.

6. **Trend & Time Intelligence Analysis:** Implemented MTD and SPLY reporting to evaluate cumulative and historical sales performance.

7. **Interactive Dashboard Development:** Designed visual reports with slicers, cross-filtering, KPI cards, geographical maps, and page navigation.

8. **Business Insights & Reporting:** Transformed retail transaction data into accessible performance reports supporting business planning.

## Key Business Insights

The dashboard analysis highlights:

- Major revenue-contributing brands, including Apple, Samsung, OnePlus, Vivo, and Xiaomi.
- High-performing mobile models, including iPhone SE, OnePlus Nord, and Galaxy Note 20.
- Monthly sales quantities generally ranging between approximately 1.45K and 1.70K units.
- July as the highest-performing month and February as the lowest-performing month by displayed sales quantity.
- Customer ratings concentrated primarily in Good and Average categories.
- A relatively balanced transaction distribution across payment methods.

*These observations reflect the displayed dashboard and may change with report filters.*
